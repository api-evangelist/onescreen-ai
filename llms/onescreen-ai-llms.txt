# OneScreen AI

> OneScreen is a data-driven out-of-home (OOH) advertising company that makes real-world advertising — billboards, transit, and place-based media — as queryable and buyable as any digital channel. It combines 1,500+ OOH audience personas, close to one million live inventory listings, and audience-based market/inventory recommendation models, and exposes that intelligence to AI agents through a public Model Context Protocol (MCP) server. OneScreen has no traditional public REST API; its agent-facing surface is the MCP server.

## Agent surface
- [OneScreen MCP Server](https://www.onescreen.ai/mcp/): Remote MCP server (HTTP, OAuth 2.1) connecting OneScreen OOH research data to Claude, ChatGPT, or any MCP client. Free public beta (access by request). See mcp/onescreen-ai-mcp.yml in this repo.

## Products
- OneScreen Research (free): audience personas, inventory listings, and market/inventory recommendations.
- OneScreen Planner (closed beta, invitation-only): higher rate limits, permission-based pricing intelligence, and planning/buying workflow integration.
